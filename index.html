Our story

We are the Al-Kurdi family. When we were forced to flee Jabalia, we left with only the clothes we wore and our identity documents. We started again from nothing, and we still cannot secure the most basic necessities, from clothing to daily food.

Dr. Mohammed, a dentist for over 25 years, used to heal others and keep a stable home. After repeated displacement from Jabalia to Rafah, then across Al-Zawayda and Gaza, he has lost his clinic and cannot carry the cost of supporting his family.

We live in a nylon tent. Our elders, like Sobhia, live with high blood pressure and have no steady medicine or care. Azhar fears every day for her children, Tamem and Asmaa, who have lost play and schooling. Hatem and Abood, two young men who want to finish university, are watching their futures slip away.

We share our stories and achievements honestly, and we believe human compassion can bridge even the darkest gaps.
